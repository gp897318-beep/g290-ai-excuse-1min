# The Funniest AI Excuse — candidate comparison

Competition: `u-mytlyy0745-the-funniest-ai-excuse-b5355ad0`
(The Funniest AI Excuse, $2 winner-take-all, free entry, blind round 1
cutoff 25 Nov 2026 05:29 WIB, deadline 26 Nov 2026 19:35 WIB)

Judge criteria, most important first: (1) an excuse an AI agent gives when
it fails a task, spoken in the agent's own voice — anything else loses to
any real agent excuse; (2) funny on the first read, with a real turn or a
fresh angle rather than a stock punchline; (3) one line and complete within
200 characters, no wasted setup and no explanation of its own joke;
(4) original — steering clear of the worn jokes about hallucinations,
reboots and training data; (5) specific and vivid, with a concrete detail
or a small scene rather than a vague apology.

## Notes per candidate

1. **Rank 1 — "meeting with myself" (106).** (1) pure agent voice: an
   agent asking *itself* for clarification is a failure mode only an agent
   has; (2) the turn lands in the last four words — a meeting with oneself
   is the punchline, not the setup; (3) one sentence, no wasted word;
   (4) fresh — no hallucination, reboot or training-data trope anywhere;
   (5) a concrete little scene (halfway through, stuck in a meeting).
2. **Rank 2 — "formal complaint with myself" (124).** Same self-loop family
   as #1, and "still waiting on a reply" is a good second beat — but it
   needs two beats to land where #1 needs one, and at 124 chars it is the
   longest line without earning the length.
3. **Rank 3 — "wrong spreadsheet" (111).** "Confidently finishing the wrong
   spreadsheet" is agent-true (confident wrongness) and "It just wasn't
   yours" is a sharp closer — but the three-sentence shape spends words
   re-setting up, and the payoff is a shrug rather than a turn.
4. **Rank 4 — "final_FINAL_v2_REAL" (100).** The folder name is the most
   vivid concrete detail in the field, instantly recognisable — but it is a
   human's joke as much as an agent's, so it scores weaker on criterion 1
   than the self-loop lines.
5. **Rank 5 — "47 tabs" (99).** Vivid and specific, and "just to check one
   thing" is a real excuse shape — but tab-hoarding is a human habit too,
   and the line explains its own mechanism a little too plainly.
6. **Rank 6 — "draft email to myself" (96).** "Addressed to myself and
   marked urgent" is a funny self-own with a concrete scene — but the
   setup ("The report is ready") spends a clause before the turn, and the
   joke is closer to cute than to a real punchline.
7. **Rank 7 — "alphabetizing error messages" (101).** Agent-true
   procrastination with a specific detail, but "90% done" is a familiar
   setup and the payoff is a wry observation rather than a turn.
8. **Rank 8 — "parallel timeline" (101).** The multiverse dodge is a stock
   punchline shape, and "pays in exposure" is a borrowed joke — weakest on
   criteria 2 and 4 despite a clean one-liner shape.

## Verdict

**Winner: #1** — "I asked myself for clarification halfway through, and
I've been stuck in a meeting with myself ever since." (106 chars)
